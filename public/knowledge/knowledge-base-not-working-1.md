pressure, diabetics, heart conditions, or any other condition and are taking medication for those issues.

What do you mean by stable?
&quot;Stable” or “Stability&quot; means that during the period selected in Your Application for Insurance,
immediately preceding Your Effective Date Your condition is not worsening and there has been: :
no hospitalization; and no new diagnosis, treatment or prescribed medication; and no change,
reduction, increase or stoppage in medication dosage or its frequency. no new, more frequent or more
severe symptoms; and no new test results showing deterioration; and no referral to a specialist (made
or recommended) and you are not awaiting surgery or the results of further investigations performed by
any medical professional.
